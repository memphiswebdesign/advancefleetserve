  <section class="job-posting__responsibilities">
    <h4 class="mb-3">What You’ll Do:</h4>
    <ul class="markers gap-sm">
      <li>Perform DOT-compliant federal annual inspections and preventative maintenance</li>
      <li>Diagnose and repair ABS, brake, and wheel-end systems</li>
      <li>Perform tire changes, repairs, and related service</li>
      <li>Troubleshoot and repair electrical and air systems</li>
      <li>Perform trailer body repairs and rebuilds based on experience and skill level</li>
      <li>Repair trailer doors, door panels, operators, and landing gear</li>
      <li>Install decals and complete other trailer repair and maintenance tasks</li>
      <li>Safely operate shop equipment, power tools, hand tools, forklifts, torches, and welding equipment</li>
      <li>Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work environment</li>
      <li>Accurately record labor, production time, and completed work</li>
      <li>Follow company safety procedures and quality standards</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="job-posting__requirements">
    <h4 class="mb-3">Qualifications:</h4>
    <ul class="markers gap-sm">
      <li>2+ years of mechanical experience required</li>
      <li>Experience with heavy-duty equipment, trailers, fleet maintenance, or commercial vehicles preferred</li>
      <li>Valid driver’s license required</li>
      <li>Experience with brakes, ABS, wheel ends, electrical systems, and air systems preferred</li>
      <li>Ability to operate a forklift</li>
      <li>Ability to safely use a torch and welding equipment</li>
      <li>Ability to use a variety of power and hand tools</li>
      <li>Ability to lift up to 50 pounds</li>
      <li>Strong troubleshooting and mechanical skills</li>
      <li>Reliable, safety-minded, and detail-oriented</li>
      <li>Willingness to learn and take on additional responsibilities</li>
    </ul>
